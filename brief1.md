### 1b. Fees, visa facilitation agreements (VFAs) and Art. 25a measures
- Standard fee is EUR 90 for adults and EUR 45 for children aged 6-11. Art. 16 allows EUR 135 or 180 if a Council decision is adopted under Art. 25a(5)(b) [S1].
- No Art. 25a measure was found in force for any of the 11 passports. The Art. 25a cases found were Gambia (higher fee repealed in 2024, but 45-day processing and no multiple-entry visas remain per secondary reports) [S7] and Ethiopia (measures lifted 18 May 2026) [S8]. That none of the 11 is affected is **needs verification** against the Council register.
- VFAs: none was found for the 11 passports. China, India and Pakistan have no EU VFA as far as I could tell (**needs verification**). Any further reductions (e.g. for students) fall under Art. 16 generally [S1].

### 1c. Multiple-entry cascade
- **India:** a 2-year multiple-entry visa (MEV) after two visas lawfully used in the previous 3 years. This applies **only to Indians residing in and applying in India** [S4], so it does **not** apply to UK-resident applicants.
- **Ghana:** a 3-year MEV after one lawfully used 2-year MEV within 3 years. This applies to applications lodged in Ghana [S5].
- **Türkiye:** 6 months, then 1 year, then 3 years, then 5 years, for Turkish nationals residing in Türkiye (truck drivers excluded), from 15 July 2025 [S6]. The primary text is **needs verification**.
- **Everyone else, and all UK-lodged applications:** the general Art. 24(2) cascade, i.e. 1 year, then 2 years, then 5 years [S1][S6]. Whether other per-country decisions exist (e.g. China, Philippines, Thailand, South Africa) is **needs verification**.

## Part 2. Application form field mapping and UK application paths
The harmonised form is Annex I of the Visa Code (fields 1-37 in the 2020 version) [S1]. **I could not obtain the portal labels.** France-Visas, VIDEX and the German Consular Services Portal, and Italy's form are all behind login or dynamic forms. A field-by-field mapping is therefore **needs verification** for all three countries. The Annex I field headings are: 1 surname; 2 surname at birth; 3 first names; 4 date of birth; 5 place of birth; 6 country of birth; 7 current nationality; 8 sex; 9 civil status; 10 parental authority (minors); 11 national ID number; 12 travel document type; 13 document number; 14 issue date; 15 valid until; 16 issued by; 17 EU/EEA/CH family member details; 18 family relationship; 19 home address/email/phone; 20 residence in another country (UK permit number and validity); 21 current occupation; 22 employer/school; 23 purpose; 24 additional info on purpose; 25 main destination; 26 first entry state; 27 number of entries; 28 fingerprints collected previously; 29 entry permit for final destination; 30 inviting person or hotel; 31 inviting company; 32 cost of travel and means of support; 33-37 signature and declarations. These headings are from memory of the 2020 Annex I and were not re-extracted, so **needs verification** [S1].

## Part 3. Application journey (FR / DE / IT)
Common steps: fill in the form, book an appointment, prepare documents, attend for biometrics, pay fees, wait for a decision, collect passport. Usual documents are passport, proof of UK residence, travel insurance of at least EUR 30,000 [S14], proof of means, itinerary and accommodation. Each consulate's own checklist is **needs verification**.
