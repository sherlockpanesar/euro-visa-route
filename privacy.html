Privacy note

What we collect: only the email address you enter in the waitlist form, plus a short summary of the route you were shown (passport type, UK status category, destinations and dates). The route checker itself runs entirely in your browser and sends nothing.

Why: to email you when pack checks launch. The lawful basis is your consent under UK GDPR. We do not sell or share your data, and we send no marketing beyond this.

How: submissions are delivered by email to the site owner via FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), a form-forwarding service.

Retention and your rights: we keep your email only until launch, or until you ask us to delete it. You can ask for access, correction or deletion, or withdraw consent at any time. Unsubscribe links will be included in every email.

Contact: contact the site owner via the GitHub repository sherlockpanesar/euro-visa-route (open an issue asking us to delete your data; don't post your email publicly) or reply to any email we send. You can also complain to the ICO (ico.org.uk).
